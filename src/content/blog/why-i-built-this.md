Every trip I've taken started as a spreadsheet. One tab for the itinerary, one for the budget, a packing list I copied from the last trip, and a "places to eat" tab full of names someone recommended. Some trips only exist as an email thread with a travel agent from 2017.

Friends kept asking for "that Vietnam sheet" or "the Andaman plan", so I decided to put everything in one place:

- **Every itinerary, day by day**, so you can copy it as it is or use it as a starting point.
- **Real budgets**, converted to your currency at live rates. Where I didn't record a cost, it's marked as an estimate.
- **The hotels, restaurants, activities and local operators** I'd recommend, plus a wishlist of things I didn't get to.
- **A planner** that takes any of my trips and adjusts it for your group size, number of days and travel style.

It's also for me. It's nice to scroll back through a timeline and remember the Andaman scuba course, the balloons over Cappadocia, and the Red Sea liveaboard.

> If you use one of these itineraries, tell me how it went. I'd love to know what you changed.
